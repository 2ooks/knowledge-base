# Are 40% Staff Cuts the New AI Normal?

**Source:** [AI Daily Brief](https://aidailybrief.beehiiv.com/p/are-40-staff-cuts-the-new-ai-normal)

## Why this matters

- **Thesis match:** Consulting-services disruption is gradual — B2B2E play, Two-tier legacy modernization — build vs partner decided by tier
- **Key take(s):**
  - Legacy modernization with AI will destroy over $1 trillion in tech ecosystem value through 2026. _(conf 0.7)_
- **Confidence:** 0.7

## Summary

Jack Dorsey announced a 40% headcount reduction at Block — roughly 4,000 employees — making it the largest tech layoff on a percentage basis in recent years and one of the first to explicitly cite AI as the trigger. Block's stock rose 25% on the news. Dorsey carefully avoided the word "AI" in the memo itself, framing the cut around an order-of-magnitude capability jump in late 2025 that "really shown a path forward in terms of us being able to apply it to nearly every single thing that we do." Block's internal agent, Goose, originally built for coding, has been deployed across non-technical teams.

Skeptics question whether AI is the real driver. Block tripled headcount from 3,900 to 12,500 between 2019 and 2022; Robinhood runs at 2,500 employees with a $70B market cap and Coinbase at 4,500 with $50B. Bond investor Will Slaughter called it "unwinding an insane COVID overhiring binge," and the Amazon, Duolingo, and Klarna precedents all featured CEOs framing layoffs around AI before quietly walking the connection back. Dorsey himself acknowledged the overhiring while arguing Block now targets $2M+ gross profit per person — 4× pre-COVID efficiency.

The episode also covers IBM losing 13% on the back of an Anthropic blog post about Claude modernizing legacy COBOL — its largest single-day drawdown since March 2020 — even though Anthropic first demoed the capability three months earlier and OpenAI's Morgan Stanley COBOL work was profiled the prior June. The point: markets and CEOs are now catching up on more than a year of AI advancement at once, repricing everything.

The narrative-driving takeaway: it no longer matters whether Block can actually find 40% productivity from AI. The narrative is believable, and Dorsey has fired the starting gun on the AI-productivity race.
