# How Apple's AI Strategy Changes with a New CEO

**Source:** [AI Daily Brief](https://aidailybrief.beehiiv.com/p/how-apple-s-ai-strategy-changes-with-a-new-ceo)

## Why this matters

- **Thesis match:** Compute death spiral — capacity commitments compound through 2030
- **Key take(s):**
  - The TSMC and ASML constraints will halt multi-tenant cloud expansion by 2027 in favor of single-infrastructure models. _(conf 0.8)_
- **Confidence:** 0.8

## Summary

Tim Cook is stepping down as Apple CEO, with hardware chief John Ternus stepping in. The episode frames Apple's AI track record as a "period of near-total inaction, followed by a high-profile promise (Apple Intelligence) that delivered essentially nothing, followed by what may have been accidental genius" as the Mac became the indispensable hardware for the agentic era. Mac Minis have sold out repeatedly as the de facto AI workstation; every major new AI feature ships Mac-first or Mac-only. Cook delivered 11× market cap growth over 15 years — but Microsoft did 14×, Amazon 28×, and Facebook 35× in the same window. Ternus is described as a Jobs-era "decisiveness" pick; Robert Scoble argues he needs to hire a Chief AI Officer as a peer immediately, not a VP.

The compute-supply story drives the Tier-1 match. TSMC reported 35% revenue growth year-over-year and raised forward growth guidance above 30%. The brief reports ASML cannot deliver lithography machines fast enough, and the memory shortage is forecast to run to at least 2027 — current production meets only 60% of demand, with some forecasters seeing the squeeze extend to 2030. Memory and lithography, not GPU dies, are now the binding constraints.

Adjacent compute moves reinforce the thesis: Amazon committed $25B to Anthropic ($5B now, $20B milestone-tied) bundled with 5 GW of Trainium capacity — functionally "Anthropic paying for chips with equity"; Meta scrapped its most advanced custom training chip and pivoted to renting Google TPUs and buying NVIDIA and AMD GPUs; Cursor is in talks for $2B at a $50B valuation with NVIDIA participating, training Composer 2.5 on xAI's compute. Google's DeepMind launched a Sergei-Brin-led "strike team" to close the coding gap with Anthropic, with Boris Cherny saying ~100% of Anthropic's code is now AI-written vs Google's ~50%.
